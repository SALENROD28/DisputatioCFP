What CCCC Provided

An Incomplete History CCCC was founded in 1949 within the National Council of Teachers of English (NCTE) by Dr. John C. Gerber. Since its incipient, the conference has met annually through 2026, when it was de-charted. CCCC published the first issue of College Composition and Communication (CCC) in 1950 which kick started a long history of research and scholarship. The journal publishes research and scholarship in rhetoric and composition studies that supports college teachers in reflecting on and improving their writing instruction. This work reflects current scholarship and theory in the field. Areas of scholarship include but not limited to, English studies, rhetoric, cultural studies, communication sociology and more. CCCC has three different publication outlets: College Composition and Communication, Studies in Writing and Rhetoric (SWR), and FORUM.
